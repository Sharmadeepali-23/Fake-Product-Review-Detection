Project Information & Capstone Overview

ReviewGuard AI – College Examination & Presentation Dossier
Problem Statement

E-commerce platforms face widespread trust erosion due to astroturfing, incentivized bot reviews, and fake competitor smear campaigns. Unchecked fake reviews mislead consumers and harm legitimate businesses.

Core Solution

ReviewGuard combines TF-IDF lexical frequency representation with Logistic Regression and heuristic anomaly detection rules, providing transparent explainability tokens to human trust-and-safety moderators.

Key Deliverables

Full interactive presentation dashboard, single review inspector with NLP signal explainability, batch CSV file analysis, and comparative machine learning metric evaluation.

5-Stage AI System Architecture Workflow
1
Data Ingestion

Single text input or batch CSV dataset upload.

Cleaning, tokenization, lowercasing, and punctuation stripping.

3

Extracting unigrams & bigrams with term frequency weights.

Viva Presentation Q&A Guide (Examiner FAQ)

Standard questions frequently asked by project evaluation committees

Answer: While deep transformer models like BERT achieve high accuracy, Logistic Regression paired with TF-IDF offers three critical practical advantages for e-commerce review monitoring:
  • Explainability (XAI): Linear coefficients directly tell us which words increased suspicion (e.g. "miracle", "1000%") vs which words proved authenticity (e.g. "battery", "after weeks"). Deep networks are black boxes.
  • Computational Efficiency: Inference takes sub-milliseconds per review, allowing platforms to scan millions of batch reviews without expensive GPU infrastructure.
  • Resilience to Overfitting: L2 regularization on TF-IDF sparse matrices handles noisy product text robustly.

Answer: TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) measures how important a word is to a document relative to an entire corpus. Common filler words (e.g., "the", "and") receive low weights. However, specific astroturfing patterns (e.g., "god tier", "1000% recommend", "stole my money") have high specificity in spam reviews. Conversely, genuine reviews contain high term frequency for product-specific nouns and temporal usage verbs.

Answer: Automated NLP models cannot inspect the real physical purchase receipt. Sarcasm, cultural idioms, and genuine consumers who write short, highly enthusiastic reviews may trigger false positives. Therefore, ReviewGuard is designed as a triage assistance tool for human moderators rather than an autonomous judge that deletes user reviews.

Answer: In real-world marketplaces, genuine reviews outnumber fake reviews (often 80/20). In machine learning, this is mitigated through balanced class weighting (class_weight='balanced'), evaluating using the F1-Score and Precision-Recall curve rather than accuracy alone, and employing SMOTE or cost-sensitive learning.
